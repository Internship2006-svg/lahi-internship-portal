👋 Welcome to Your Internship Journey!

An internship is a chance to work at a real workplace for a short period of time. You get to try out a job, see how things work, and learn skills that you can use in the future.

You are not expected to know everything. You are here to learn.

Say this to yourself every day:
  • I am ready for today.
  • I come on time, every day.
  • I watch, listen and ask questions.
  • I am here to learn.
  • I am kind to everyone around me.
  • I can do this!

🌟 What Can You Gain From an Internship?

💼 Real Work ExperienceExperience how a real workplace functions.
🧭 Career UnderstandingLearn more about different jobs and careers.
🔎 Explore Your InterestsDiscover what kind of work you enjoy.
🛠️ Hands-on LearningLearn by observing and doing real tasks.
⭐ Employability SkillsBuild communication, teamwork, initiative and other workplace skills.

👨‍👩‍👧 Talk to Your Parent / Guardian

Before starting your internship, talk to your parent or guardian. Make sure they understand where you will go, what you will do and when your internship will take place.

📋 Consent and Confirmation

Before starting your internship, make sure the required consent and confirmation process has been completed.

🛡️ Know the Safety Basics

Safety is one of the most important parts of your internship. Pay attention to the workplace and ask questions if you are unsure.

Find out where these are:
  • First-aid kit
  • Fire exit
  • Safety equipment
  • Important safety instructions
  • Person to contact if something feels unsafe
