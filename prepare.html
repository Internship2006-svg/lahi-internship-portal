MISSION 1 • UNDERSTAND YOUR INTERNSHIP

🎒 Prepare for Your Internship

Before you begin your internship, make sure you understand where you are going, what you will do, how to stay safe, and who can support you.

🚀 Before You Begin

An internship is an opportunity to experience a real workplace, learn from people around you and understand what it means to work in a particular sector or job role.

Before starting, complete the preparation activities below. They will help you enter your internship with confidence.

Remember: Your internship is not only about completing tasks. It is also about observing, asking questions, learning, working with others and reflecting on your experience.

1️⃣ Talk to Your Parent / Guardian

Talk to your parent or guardian before starting your internship. Make sure they know where you will go, what you will be doing and how long the internship will last.

2️⃣ Complete the Consent and Confirmation

Before starting the internship, make sure the required consent and confirmation process is completed.

3️⃣ Know Your Internship Sector

Internships can happen across different sectors. Understand which sector your internship belongs to and what kind of work happens there.

4️⃣ Know Your Internship Details

Before your first day, make sure you know the basic details of your internship.

🛡️ 5️⃣ Know the Safety Basics

Safety comes first. On your first day, pay attention to the workplace safety arrangements.

On your first day, find out:
  • Where the first-aid kit is located.
  • What safety rules you need to follow.
  • Which tools or equipment require special care.
  • Where the fire exit is located.
  • Who you should contact if something feels unsafe.

✅ Your Ready-to-Start Checklist

Before you start the mission, check that you have understood the preparation points above.

Tip: If you are unsure about anything, ask your teacher, Vocational Trainer, parent / guardian or employer before beginning the internship.
