FIRST-TIME GUIDE
BEFORE YOU START

Welcome! Your internship adventure starts here. 👋

Before you jump into Mission 1, take one minute to understand what you will do, what you will fill, and what you should keep checking during your internship. Think of this as your quick-start map.

1

🛡️ Get ready

Talk to your parent/guardian, get the required documents and understand the safety guidelines.

2

🏪 Know your workplace

Know your sector, workplace and mentor. Introduce yourself and be ready to learn.

3

👀 Observe & record

Notice people, tasks, tools, machines, skills and safety practices before rushing into the work.

4

🎯 Set goals

Turn your observations into learning goals and smaller steps you can track.

What will I fill in?

You can come back to these activities whenever you need help.

Mission 1 — PrepareParent/guardian readiness, internship details, sector, workplace/mentor details and safety preparation.
Mission 2 — ExploreWorkplace details, at least 4 people you meet, tools/machines, skills you notice and safety observations.
Mission 3 — Set Your GoalsWhat you want to learn, why it matters, how you will know you are improving and your sub-goals.
Mission 4 — Learn BetterDaily reflections, new knowledge, learning techniques, badges, self-assessment, future pathway and employer feedback.
💡 Your rule for the whole journey: Watch. Listen. Ask. Try. Record. You do not need perfect sentences. Short notes, summaries, word maps and drawings are all useful ways to capture what you learn.
🎮
And yes — there are games!Complete learning challenges to collect badges, XP and your Learn Better Champion Trophy.
